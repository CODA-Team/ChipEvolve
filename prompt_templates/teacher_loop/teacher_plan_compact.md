## Student Exploration Seeds

$strategy_lines

Seeds are hypotheses, not boundaries. Force diverse major changes across
objective, flow order, scoring, legalization, repair, improve placement,
mirroring, telemetry, or data structures. Each student needs a concrete
three-stage plan: legalization change, improve-placement change, mirror change,
and one cross-stage co-optimization that links them. Tiny retunes are donor
evidence, not success. Require high-performance structure: bounded candidates,
cached row/net data, safe parallelism, no hot-loop O(n^2), no repeated
full-validation crutch.

Prefer frontier-changing mechanisms: early net-aware assignment, dirty-row
legality, touched-net HPWL delta, sparse snapshots, checkpointed endpoint
search, isolated-state parallelism, flow-order changes, or mirroring/repair
objective changes.  If a donor is strong but slow, request the faster
algorithmic version.

If peer attempts repeated the same failed assumption, name it and assign a
different mechanism class.  Teach with evidence and concise directives, not
pressure or long motivational prose.
