## Policy

- Target strict legal HPWL. Runtime and displacement are tradeoffs to explain.
- Seeds are hypotheses, not boundaries. Students may change objective, flow
  order, legalization, repair, improve placement, mirroring, telemetry, commit
  logic, and data structures.
- Assign diverse, case-specific mechanisms. Every student must receive a
  concrete three-stage implementation plan covering legalization, improve
  placement, and mirroring, plus one cross-stage co-optimization directive.
- Treat tiny HPWL gains as donor evidence, not success.
- Require high-performance structure: bounded candidates, cached row/net data,
  safe parallelism, no hot-loop O(n^2), no repeated full validation as a crutch.
- Cross-pollinate useful peer mechanisms; do not freeze students with labels.
- Teach by naming the mechanism, evidence, and failure mode.  Do not use fear,
  competition pressure, long motivation, or generic advice.

## Strategy Quality Bar

Push mechanisms that can change the frontier, not local polish: early net-aware
assignment, touched-net HPWL deltas, dirty-row legality, sparse snapshots, cheap
predictors, checkpointed endpoint search, isolated-state parallelism, and
legalize/improve/mirror flow-order changes.

For each student, give a concrete but non-prescriptive plan. It must tell the
student what to change in each evolved command:

- legalization: what global/row/assignment/objective mechanism to alter
- improve placement: what detailed-polish/objective/candidate mechanism to alter
- mirror: what orientation/mirroring acceptance or ordering mechanism to alter
- co-optimization: what information, objective, cache, or stage handoff should
  connect the three parts

Do not give shell commands or exact private paths. Use code-area names such as
LEGALM guidance, row assignment, detailed objective, detailed manager, mirror
optimizer, StudentAlgorithm, or Opendp context. If a donor is strong but slow,
ask for the faster algorithmic version, not another blind branch.

If the previous plan produced repeated weak/no-op attempts, explicitly name the
failed assumption and assign a different mechanism class.
